Questions

Common questions about Marieliez

Straight answers about our branches, service areas, ordering, and urgent care.

Where are your branches?

Marieliez Pharmacy has three Cairo locations: Al Golf Branch at 23 Ahmed Tayseer St., Al Golf, Nasr City (+20 128 678 9937); Highland Park Branch at Highland Park Mall, El Andalus, New Cairo (+20 112 111 1600); and El Katameya Branch in El Katameya, New Cairo (+20 110 805 7225). Site-wide WhatsApp orders use +20 112 111 1600.

Which areas and compounds do you serve?

We serve Nasr City and New Cairo / East Cairo neighbourhoods, including communities around Highland Park such as Akoya, SODIC Eastown, Villette, Kattameya Plaza, and EDNC. Message us on WhatsApp to confirm delivery for your compound.

How do I order online?

Browse the product catalog, add items to your cart, then send the order on WhatsApp. You can also message +20 112 111 1600 with a prescription or product list.

What about urgent or emergency orders?

Urgent orders are prioritized. Contact us right away by WhatsApp or phone so the pharmacy team can help quickly.

Which beauty brands do you carry?

We stock dermocosmetics and haircare from Vichy, Kérastase, La Roche-Posay, L'Oréal, and similar lines. Ask a pharmacist in store for guidance.
